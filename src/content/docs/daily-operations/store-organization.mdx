At least two of the staff members are always on-site in the main store area. One of the staff members is responsible for the register, and the other has to keep the store area as organized, clean and tidy as possible at all times. On the busier days, the store manager might ask another person to be present in the store area from the preparing team to maintain the shop's condition. If you are responsible for the store maintenance, please complete the following tasks during your shift.

## Clearing the fitting rooms and the fitting room rack

Have a look on the rack near the fitting room in every 10-15 minutes. If the clothes are stacking up on the rack, proceed with the following steps.

<Steps>

1. Go to the rack and arrange the clothes properly on the appropriate type of hangers.

2. Check the fitting rooms for further items and hangers. Customers tend to leave a lot of stuff behind the curtains, so brace yourself, especially on a busier day.

3. Button and zip up all the zippable and buttonable items (pants, skirts, shirts, blouses, jackets, etc).

4. Have a quick quality check on the items, look for any staining or damages (makeup stains, ripped seams, broken zippers). If you spot any, place the damaged item temporarily on the rack near the register.

5. Place the undamaged ones back on the designated store area racks. If you are unsure where you should put an item, ask for assistance.

6. Write a short paper note for the damaged items left near the register about the type of the damage, place them on the neck of the hangers and bring themű items back to the ironing room. Check the [damage handling guide](#) (TBA) for further instructions.

</Steps>

<Aside title="Mind the colors">
  Occasionally, we not only sort the items by category, but also we try to
  create some subsets of color / pattern / material. When you put the clothes
  back, please try to pay attention and preserve this kind of sorting on the
  racks as well.
</Aside>

## Organising the store area items

Look around the store area in every 10-15 minutes, and check or fix the followings.

- Check the floor for any garments that fell off the hangers, and place them back. If you can't find it's empty hanger on the rack, get one from the rack near the register or from the ironing room.

- Check the straight hangers and untangle them if they look messy, tangled or stuck. All the hangers should look organized and in order.

- If the racks look sparsed and thin, ask the store manager if you can fill them up from the stock. If they say so, fill up the rack, but **don't overcrowd them, leave room for the clothes to breathe**. The rack should look uncluttered, organised and easily browsable.

- On the other hand, when a rack looks overstuffed, remove some items from it and bring them back to the stock in the ironing room.

<Aside type="caution" title="Keep the back stock organized!">
  When you place any items in the stock, make sure that you put the items under
  the proper category. Check for any labels matching the category you intend to
  place in the stock. If you can't find any, write a new one using the white
  plastic cards and the colored IKEA markers placed on the big white table in
  the ironing room. **NEVER** use permanent markers on the white plastic cards.
</Aside>

- Check the accessories - bag shelves, glass cabinets, shoe shelves, bandana / scarf luggages - regularly. If they look cluttered, organise them. If they look sparse, fill them up from the back stock. If there is no priced items to put out in the store area, inform the store manager and wait for further instructions.

- Have a look at the cleaning sheet, and complete the scheduled task for the day proactively. If the upcoming task is the kitchen or the storage room cleaning, ask the store manager whether you are allowed to leave your place or should someone else complete the task. After you finished, don't forget to fill your name in the appropriate cell on the sheet.

- Button up / zip up the garments regularly. On a busy day, it is enough to fix only one button. But if you can find the time for it, try to button / zip up the whole rack of clothes.

In summary, try to maintain the overal clean and organised feel of the shop.
