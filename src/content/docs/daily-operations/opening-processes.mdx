## Opening Shift Tasks (10:00 AM)

These are the tasks for the staff member on the opening shift, who arrives by **10:00 AM**.

<Steps>
1. Deactivate the alarm.

2. Check that you closed the back door behind you **properly**.

3. Turn on the power to the shop (flip the switches marked with the **brown label** on the electrical panel in the ironing room).

4. Check if the air conditioners are working properly. If any controller or indoor unit displays an error message, or if you notice any other issue, **report to Loi immediately.**

5. Check the cleaning sheet on the door of the ironing room to see which section of the shop needs to be mopped that day.

6. Vacuum the area indicated on the cleaning sheet.

7. Prepare the mopping machine and mop the area.

</Steps>

<Aside>
  Start the mopping with the entrance and the register area every day, then
  proceed with the area indicated on the sheet.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Mind the fitting room">
  On men's day, don't forget to mop the fitting room area as well!
</Aside>

<Card title="How to prepare and clean the mopping machine?">

<Steps>

1. Remove the water tanks from the front of the machine.

2. Pour the dirty water down the drain in front of the washing machine on the floor.

3. Then, pour some mopping liquid in the clean water tank and fill up the tank with clean water by the wall tap next to the washing machine.

4. Put everything back in place and start mopping

</Steps>

<Aside title="Strange noises">
  The mopping machine sometimes gives off strange sounds, which can be
  considered normal. In case the machine sounds **unusually strange and squeaky,
  or it doesn't mop the floor properly, and leaks the dirty water back on the
  floor**, there might be an issue with the rubber bands on the cleaner head. In
  this case, please **let the store manager know** that the rubber bands might
  have to be cleaned or changed.
</Aside>

</Card>

<Aside type="caution" title="Check the gums">
  Occasionally, customers tend to spit their chewing gums on the floor. During
  the opening sessions, please check the floors for chewing gum residues, and if
  you find any, remove them with the spatula tool, which can be found in the
  white box under the register desk.
</Aside>

## Regular Shift Tasks (10:30 AM)

These are the tasks for the staff members on the regular shift, who arrive by **10:30AM**.

<Steps>
1. Based on the Store Manager's instructions and mutual agreement, one person vacuums the rest of the store areas, while the others organize the store.

2. Restock inventory as directed by the Store Manager.

3. _Task for senior staff members or the store managers:_ Count the till and complete the opening procedures on the NAV register. If there is too much cash in the till after counting (an amount that makes hard to handle and count the money), please contact Loi and wait for further instructions, before proceeding with the NAV opening process.

4. Put the vacuum and mopping machine back in their places, and wrap the extension cords.

5. Personal items must not be left on the metal shelving unit behind the register. Before opening, please store all your belongings in the small Kallax cabinet next to the register, in the kitchen, or in the shared changing area.

6. If the music is off, turn it on using the company phone located by the register. In case the speaker has disconnected from the phone, check this [troubleshooting guide](#) (TBA).

7. Check if the rugs have been properly put back in place after mopping.

8. Close the door of the ironing room, the laundry room and the kitchen, and fix the curtain of the landry room if needed.

9. At **11:00 AM**, unlock the door using the key with the red tag, flip the sign to "Open," and place the key in the small drawer under the register.

</Steps>
