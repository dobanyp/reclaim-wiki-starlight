## Shifting Schedule

Please fill out the monthly shifting schedule sheet until the 18th of every month to claim your preferred holidays and day-offs for the next month towards the management. (i.e.: you need to fill out the February sheet until January 18, etc.) In other words:

**The sheet for month “M” will be closed on M-1 16th 20:00! No changes will be accepted after the deadline.**
