Welcome to the Reclaim Team! In this article, we have collected the most important information for you to make your onboarding experience as smooth as possible. Since we are open to work with kind people from all around the world, our official working language is English. If you have any doubts about whether you have a clear understanding of the topics covered by these guides, please feel free to reach out to any of your new colleagues.

## What is Reclaim Vintage?

**Reclaim Vintage** is a Budapest-based curated vintage clothing shop founded by Loi Bao Nguyen and Dorka Bognár in 2023. We collect and curate high-quality vintage garments from all around the world, focusing on the Netherlands, Germany, and Italy. Alongside apparel, our space features hand-picked, pre-loved home decor items, sunglasses, and handmade enamel jewelry by a local designer. In February 2026, we relocated to a more spacious and brighter shop at Király utca 35-37.

## Key Roles and Contact Points

On a day-to-day basis, a store manager is always on-site alongside the store assistants. They are your first line of contact: for daily operations and routine work questions (as well as your other colleagues!). If a situation falls outside of their scope, they will contact the general managmenet or guide you on how to do so.

## Working Hours and Shifting

- Store opening hours: 11:00 - 20:00 | Mon-Sun
- Shifts:
  - Opening shift: 10:00 - 19:00 (9 hrs)
  - Regular shift: 10:30 - 20:00 (9,5 hrs)

During your first few weeks and onboarding period, you will most likely work only regular shifts. Our shifts are scheduled on a monthly basis and finalized at least two weeks before the start of each month. Please visit the [Scheduling Guide](#) to learn more about the shift scheduling system. You also have paid holidays, which you can use according to the [Holiday Guide](#).
